Privacy Policy for Sleep Sounds

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Quick Summary: Sleep Sounds does not require an account and does not upload your saved mixes, app preferences, or other in-app choices to TinySpark Apps' servers. These details are stored on your device. The app uses Google AdMob to show ads and Google Play to process purchases, and Google may process information as described below.

1. Information You Provide

Sleep Sounds does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, or contact details. The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, or location.

The app lets you save named sound mixes, including the name you give each mix, the sounds in it, and their volume levels, and choose a background wallpaper. These details are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or upload them to our servers.

Information the App Stores on Your Device

Along with your saved mixes and wallpaper choice, the app stores the following details on your device so that it can work properly:

  • Whether you have unlocked PRO, and when any temporary PRO access earned by watching a rewarded video ad expires.
  • Your total time using the app, how many times a rating prompt has been shown, and whether you followed it. These are used only to decide when to ask you to rate the app.

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your device and Google Account settings.

2. Information and Network Connections

We do not operate a service that collects or stores your saved mixes, preferences, or other in-app choices. Sleep Sounds connects to the internet for the purposes described below. As with any internet connection, the servers and network providers involved may receive basic connection information, such as your IP address.

Update and Connectivity Checks

The app requests a public app-version file from TinySpark Apps to check whether an update is available and whether your device is online. This request does not include the information stored in the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app connects to Google AdMob to request and display banner, full-screen, and optional rewarded video advertisements. AdMob may collect or receive information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized. This processing is performed by Google under its own privacy practices and is separate from the information stored by Sleep Sounds. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app uses Google's consent tools to ask for your choices about advertising before ads are requested.

Purchases (Google Play)

Sleep Sounds offers an optional one-time PRO purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment and your Google Account details under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app asks Google Play whether you own PRO so that it can unlock and restore your purchase.

Sound Downloads (Google Play)

PRO sound files are delivered through Google Play as a download to your device. This download is handled by Google Play and does not include the information stored in the app.

Links to Google Play

Links to rate the app, update it, or view other TinySpark Apps titles open the Google Play Store, which handles any information it receives under Google's privacy practices.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Information stored on your device is used to provide app features and remember your choices.
  • The app-version request is used to check whether an update is available and whether your device is online.
  • AdMob information is used by Google to provide and support advertisements, including applicable personalization, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Purchase information is used by Google to process your purchase, and by the app to confirm that you own PRO.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention, Deletion, and Your Choices

Locally stored information remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. You can delete saved mixes within the app, or clear the app's data or uninstall it through your device settings. TinySpark Apps does not receive or retain this locally stored content. Your PRO purchase stays linked to your Google Account and can be restored after you reinstall the app.

You also have the following choices about advertising:

  • Where consent is required, you can review or change your choices at any time from Ad privacy options in the app's Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, through your device or Google Account settings.
  • Buying PRO removes ads from the app. Temporary PRO access earned from a rewarded video ad does not remove ads.

5. Children's Privacy

Sleep Sounds is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers. AdMob may process information as described in Google's privacy documentation and according to the applicable settings and requirements.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated revision date.
