Privacy Policy for QR Scanner & QR Generator

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Quick Summary: QR Scanner & QR Generator does not require an account. Codes are scanned on your device, and your scan history, created codes, notes, and settings are stored only on your device. TinySpark Apps does not operate servers that collect this information. The app uses Google AdMob to show ads and Google Play to process purchases, and Google may process information as described below.

1. Information You Provide and Device Permissions

QR Scanner & QR Generator does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details. The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, or location.

Camera

The app asks for camera access so that it can scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed on your device to read codes. They are not saved, and they are not sent to TinySpark Apps. You can deny camera access and still create codes or scan codes from images.

Photos and Images

  • Scan from image: when you choose an image to scan, the app reads only that image, on your device.
  • Contact photo: when you add a photo to a contact QR code, the photo you choose is resized and placed inside the code's content. Anyone who scans that code can see the photo.
  • Save to gallery: when you save a code as an image, the app writes it to your device's photo gallery. On Android 10 and earlier, this needs storage permission.

Wi-Fi

When you choose to join a network from a scanned Wi-Fi QR code, the app uses Android's Wi-Fi settings to connect with the network name and password in that code. The app does not scan for nearby networks and does not use your location.

Contacts, Calendar, and Other Apps

The app does not read or write your contacts or calendar directly. When you choose to add a scanned contact or event, its details are handed to your contacts or calendar app, where you decide whether to save them. Similarly, phone numbers, email addresses, text messages, and map locations from a code are opened in the app you choose.

Clipboard and Shared Text

The app reads your clipboard only when you tap the option to create a code from clipboard content. If you share text to the app from another app, that text is used to create a code.

Information the App Stores on Your Device

The app stores the following on your device so that it can work properly:

  • History: the content and type of each code you scan or create, its barcode format, the date and time, whether you marked it as a favorite, and any title or notes you add.
  • Settings: your scanning choices (such as continuous scanning, duplicate handling, manual confirmation, and opening websites automatically), any custom search URL you enter, and your theme.
  • PRO status: whether you have unlocked PRO, so that ads stay off while your device is offline.

When you share, export, or print a code or your history, the app may create a temporary file on your device to pass to the app you choose.

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your device and Google Account settings.

2. Information and Network Connections

We do not operate a service that collects or stores your scans, created codes, or settings. QR Scanner & QR Generator connects to the internet for the purposes described below. As with any internet connection, the servers and network providers involved may receive basic connection information, such as your IP address.

Code Scanning (Google ML Kit)

Codes are read on your device using Google ML Kit. The camera images and images you choose are not sent to Google for scanning. ML Kit may contact Google from time to time to receive updates, and it may send Google information about how the scanning feature performs and is used. This is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app connects to Google AdMob to request and display banner advertisements and full-screen advertisements. AdMob may collect or receive information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized. This processing is performed by Google under its own privacy practices and is separate from the information stored by QR Scanner & QR Generator. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app uses Google's consent tools to ask for your choices about advertising before ads are requested.

Purchases (Google Play)

QR Scanner & QR Generator offers an optional one-time PRO purchase through Google Play that removes ads. Google processes the payment and your Google Account details under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app asks Google Play whether you own PRO so that it can unlock and restore your purchase.

Opening Scanned Content and Searching

Codes you scan may contain links, products, or other content created by third parties. When you open a link, it is loaded by your browser or the website's app, and that website receives the information it normally would from a visit. If you turn on Open websites automatically, links open as soon as they are scanned.

When you choose a search option, such as searching Google, Amazon, eBay, or a custom search URL you entered, the content of the code is sent to that service as your search. These services handle that information under their own privacy policies.

Feedback by Email

If you choose Send feedback, your email app opens with our support address. We receive only what you choose to send us, and we use it only to respond to you.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Information stored on your device is used to provide app features, such as your history and favorites, and to remember your choices.
  • Camera images and images you choose are used only to read codes on your device.
  • AdMob information is used by Google to provide and support advertisements, including applicable personalization, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Purchase information is used by Google to process your purchase, and by the app to confirm that you own PRO.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention, Deletion, and Your Choices

Locally stored information remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. You can delete items from your history within the app, or clear the app's data or uninstall it through your device settings. TinySpark Apps does not receive or retain this locally stored content. Your PRO purchase stays linked to your Google Account and can be restored with Restore Purchases after you reinstall the app.

You can turn camera, photo, or storage access on or off at any time in your device settings.

You also have the following choices about advertising:

  • Where consent is required, you can review or change your choices at any time from Ad privacy options in the app's Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, through your device or Google Account settings.
  • Buying PRO removes ads from the app.

5. Children's Privacy

QR Scanner & QR Generator is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers. AdMob may process information as described in Google's privacy documentation and according to the applicable settings and requirements.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated revision date.
