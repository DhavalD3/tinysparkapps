Privacy Policy for DailyPost

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Quick Summary: DailyPost does not upload your name, preferred language, photos, or other content you provide to TinySpark Apps' servers. These details are stored on your device. The app uses Google AdMob to show ads, and Google may process information as described below.

1. Information You Provide

DailyPost lets you choose a preferred language, enter a display name, select a photo, and choose or add background images for creating status images. These details and related app preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or upload this user-provided content to our servers.

Images you create remain on your device unless you choose to save or share them using your device's features. If you share an image, it is handled by the app or service you select.

2. Information and Network Connections

We do not operate a service that collects or stores your name, language, photos, generated images, or other content you provide in DailyPost. The app may connect to the internet to request public app-version information from TinySpark Apps so it can check whether an update is available. This request does not include the user-provided information stored in the app. As with any internet connection, the server and network providers may receive basic connection information, such as your IP address.

The app also connects to Google AdMob to request and display advertisements. AdMob may collect or receive information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. This processing is performed by Google under its own privacy practices and is separate from user content stored by DailyPost. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Information stored on your device is used to provide app features and remember your choices.
  • The app-version request is used to check whether an update is available.
  • AdMob information is used by Google to provide and support advertisements, including applicable measurement and fraud prevention.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. You can change or remove saved details within the app where those options are available, or clear the app's data or uninstall it through your device settings. TinySpark Apps does not receive or retain this locally stored content.

5. Children's Privacy

DailyPost is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers. AdMob may process information as described in Google's privacy documentation and according to the applicable settings and requirements.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated revision date.
